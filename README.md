# Republic of Dunvar — Salt-Flat Boatman's Passport

Dunvar is a salt-flat republic that floods in the rainy season: the only way across is a flat-bottom boat, and only while the water stands 30–90 cm deep. This passport belongs to Erik Halvorsen, a licensed salt-flat boatman (licence B-208) from Saltmere, and its endorsement, toll stamp and paired entry–exit stamps all follow the same rule — no crossing after heavy rain. The Marow visitor visa and the two round trips (July 2023, October 2024) are stamped in matched entry–exit pairs, all after the 09 MAY 2023 issue date.

The program copies `entry.png` (1024×1024) to the output directory.
